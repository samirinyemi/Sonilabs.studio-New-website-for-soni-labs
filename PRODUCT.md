# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Founders, startup operators, and product teams in B2B, AI, and software — typically funded, time-poor, and hiring across brand and product simultaneously. They arrive on the site evaluating studios for one of three jobs: a brand identity + marketing launch, a product UI/UX engagement, or an ongoing senior design partner.

The visitor's context is research: comparing this studio against agencies, freelancers, and other studios. They are skimming, not reading. The site has to communicate seniority and restraint within seconds, then earn the click on "Book a call."

Their job-to-be-done is to find a single trustworthy partner who can ship brand and web work without the usual agency overhead — multiple vendors, fragmented handoffs, status meetings, change orders. They are buying judgment as much as output.

## Product Purpose

Soni Labs is a Lagos-based design studio led by Samuel Irinyemi, working with a small bench of trusted collaborators across brand, motion, and engineering. The studio offers three engagements: Brand + Website (flagship, 4–6 weeks), Product Design / UI/UX (4–6 weeks, design-only handoff), and Design Partner (ongoing retainer).

The site exists to convert a small number of high-fit founders into discovery calls. Volume is not the goal; signal is. Success looks like qualified inbound — founders who already self-identified with the studio's positioning before the call, so the call is about scope and fit, not pitch.

The site doubles as a craft demonstration. If a visitor comes away unsure whether the studio has senior taste, the site has failed regardless of copy. Every section is implicit proof.

## Brand Personality

Three words: **senior, deliberate, warm.**

Voice is confident without being cold. Opinionated, but founder-friendly — the studio has a point of view about how design and build should happen (one roof, no handoffs, AI as method not service) and says so plainly. Not corporate, not flippant. The tone of a senior practitioner explaining their craft to a peer, not a vendor pitching to a buyer.

Emotional goal on first read: "this person gets it." Aspirational without being intimidating. Premium without being precious.

## Anti-references

This studio should never look or sound like:

- **AI-generated SaaS slop.** Purple gradients, neon "AI" colors, hero-metric templates, glassmorphism as default, identical card grids stacked across sections, gradient text used decoratively. The current saturated reflex for any tech-adjacent site.
- **Generic Framer / Webflow templates.** Cookie-cutter agency layouts, default everything, no point of view. The site has to feel made, not assembled.
- **Corporate consultancy aesthetic.** Navy + gray palette, stock photography, legalese tone, IBM/enterprise framing. The studio is not selling to procurement.
- **Maximalist brutalist.** Aggressive grids, clashing type, raw-HTML aesthetics. Restraint, not noise. The reference for restraint is klime.studio's discipline with backgrounds (the level of restraint to aim for, not the visual style to copy).

The site explicitly does not sell AI as a deliverable, automation, chatbots, lead-gen-while-you-sleep, or 24/7 magic. AI may surface only as a production methodology inside the Why Soni Labs and process narrative.

## Design Principles

1. **Show senior taste, don't claim it.** The site IS the proof. Copy that announces "premium quality" or "craft" without the visual delivering it is a tell. Every section has to read as evidence.
2. **Restraint over decoration.** Generous whitespace and typographic confidence carry the page. No dot-grid backgrounds, no decorative texture fills, no hero blob shapes. When in doubt, remove.
3. **Outcomes over deliverables.** Case studies lead with the problem and what shifted, not screenshots and tech stacks. "Modern, beautiful design" is banned as a description.
4. **AI is a method, not a service.** Production accelerator inside Brand + Website builds. Never a named deliverable, never a price line, never a hero claim.
5. **One studio, one relationship.** The site reflects the same low-friction promise the studio sells: no handoffs, one voice, one CTA path (Book a call). Multiple competing CTAs would contradict the pitch.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Best-effort, not formally audited against a spec. The site should:

- Maintain legible contrast in all body text and CTAs (target AA naturally, even if not certified).
- Preserve keyboard focus indicators on every interactive element.
- Use semantic HTML (proper headings, landmarks, button vs. link distinction).
- Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` for any motion that's decorative — entrance reveals, scroll triggers, scale hovers — so motion-sensitive visitors are not punished.
- Be usable with a screen reader for the core conversion path: read the pitch, find the services, book a call.

WCAG AA / AAA compliance is not a contractual target. If a specific surface needs to meet a higher bar (e.g. a future client-facing tool), it can be flagged at task time.
