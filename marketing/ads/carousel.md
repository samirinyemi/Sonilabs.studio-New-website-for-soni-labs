# Ad 3 — Carousel (LinkedIn + Instagram Feed)

**Goal:** Cold audience swipes through and ends on a booked Calendly call. Each slide must stand alone (people swipe out of order on IG); the deck as a whole must answer *who, what, how, and what's it cost me to find out*.

**Format:** 10 slides · 1080×1080 square · static · brand typography · generous whitespace · designed for both LinkedIn and IG without modification.

**Through-line:** *Built under one roof.* Every slide reinforces the wedge against the buyer's existing alternative (freelancer stack, full-service agency).

---

## Slide-by-slide copy

Every line below is final copy — no placeholders. The headline is what should dominate the slide visually. The subhead is optional supporting type if the slide needs breathing room filled.

### Slide 1 — Hook
**Headline:**
> Most startups buy design backwards.

**Subhead:**
> A logo from one freelancer. A site from another. A product designer when funding lands. Then nothing matches.

**Visual:** Type-first. Three mismatched brand swatches (different fonts, different greys) sitting awkwardly next to each other. The disagreement is the visual.

**Save-trigger:** None. This slide is hook-only.

## A/B hook variants for Slide 1

Test these against the primary hook ("Most startups buy design backwards.") over the first 2 weeks. Same deck, just slide 1 swapped.

| Variant | Hook |
|---|---|
| A (primary) | Most startups buy design backwards. |
| B | You don't need three vendors. You need one studio. |
| C | Your brand and your product don't agree. Customers can tell. |
| D | If a freelancer is your design strategy, you have a design problem. |

Variant D is the sharpest and most likely to overperform on X-style audiences; variant B is the safest LinkedIn opener.
