# Soni Labs — Website Action Plan

A section-by-section blueprint for what to change, what to cut, and what to add before launch.

---

## Navigation

Four items plus one CTA button. Nothing else.

| Nav Item | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| Services | Jumps to packaged offerings |
| Work | Case studies framed as problems solved |
| About | Who's behind the studio |
| FAQ | Handles objections before a prospect reaches out |
| **Start a Project** (button) | Primary CTA, always visible in the nav |

---

## Section 1: Hero

**Rewrite the headline.** Remove "AI-Powered" and any mention of lead generation. The real differentiator is that you design AND build from one seat with zero handoffs — lead with that.

Headline direction:

> Design, brand, and build your website — all from one studio.

Subhead direction:

> Brand strategy, web design, and development in Framer, Webflow, or Wix Studio. No handoffs between designers and developers. No months of back-and-forth. Just sharp work, shipped fast.

**Remove** the four floating corner labels (Design / Code / Automate / Convert). They dilute focus and "Automate" and "Convert" no longer apply.

**Keep** the "Built with your team, not handed off to them" badge — it's strong. Consider promoting it to the main tagline.

**Keep** the dual CTA buttons ("Start a Project" + "See Services").

---

## Section 2: Problem Statement

**Keep the core message** — "Your website should bring you clients, not just look good" is a strong opener. The pain point (hiring separate people, dealing with miscommunication, inflated costs) still holds.

**Rewrite the body copy** to remove references to "automation specialist" and "AI automation." The pain is still real without those: most businesses hire a designer, then a separate developer, and deal with delays and miscommunication. You solve that by doing both.

**Revise the three value proposition cards:**

| Current | Change to |
|---------|-----------|
| One Studio, Zero Handoffs | **Keep as-is** — strongest message on the site |
| AI Built In, Not Bolted On | **"Designed and built by the same person"** — the real differentiator is that the designer IS the developer |
| Looks Premium, Converts Like Crazy | **"Looks premium, works even harder"** — keeps the point, drops the aggressive marketing tone |

---

## Section 3: Services

**Replace the current service packages with three clean offerings:**

### Package 1: Brand Identity & Strategy

What's included:
- Brand positioning and naming
- Visual identity (logo, colour, typography)
- Brand guidelines document
- Marketing materials design

Price: $2,000–$5,000
Timeline: 2–3 weeks

### Package 2: Website Design & Build (Flagship)

This is the core offering — make it visually prominent.

What's included:
- Custom website design (UI/UX)
- Development in Framer, Webflow, or Wix Studio
- Responsive across all devices
- Motion and interactions
- Performance optimisation
- Basic form setup and integrations
- One round of revisions

Price: $3,000–$7,000
Timeline: 2–4 weeks

### Package 3: Embedded Design Partner (Retainer)

What's included:
- Ongoing UI/UX design
- Website updates and new pages
- Brand consistency across touchpoints
- Priority turnaround

Price: $2k–$4k/month
Timeline: Monthly rolling

**Remove the "Lead Gen Automation" package entirely.** If you do light form/CRM setup as part of a website build, list it as a line item inside the Website Design & Build package — not as its own service.

## Section 4: Process

**Keep the four-step structure with real timeframes.** Revise the labels and descriptions:

| Step | Duration | Description |
|------|----------|-------------|
| 01 Discovery | 2–3 days | Understand business goals, audience, and success metrics. Map out site architecture and requirements. |
| 02 Design | 3–5 days | Visual concepts grounded in your brand. High-fidelity mockups of key pages. Iterate until approved. |
| 03 Build | 5–10 days | Design becomes a live site in Framer, Webflow, or Wix Studio. You get a staging link to review the fully responsive site. |
| 04 Launch | 2–3 days | Final polish, deploy, SEO basics, forms set up. Handover and training so you can manage your own site. |

**Change "Launch & Automate" to just "Launch."** Remove "automated email sequences" and "CRM integrations" as headline features. If you do those things for a client, mention them in conversation — they don't need to be on the site.

---

## Section 5: Work / Case Studies

**Replace the placeholder "Selected Work" section with real projects:** AMA Victoria, BMX, meCash.

**Frame each project as a problem solved, not a portfolio piece.** For each:

- **Client:** Who they are (one line)
- **Challenge:** What problem they needed solved
- **What we delivered:** Services used (e.g., "Brand identity + website design & build in Framer")
- **Result:** Outcome or current status
- **Visual:** Screenshot or mockup

This is the difference between a portfolio ("look what I made") and a business site ("here's a problem I solved for someone like you").

---

## Section 6: About / The Studio

**Keep the personal identification** — you're a solo studio, and clients are hiring you. Own that.

Suggested structure:

- Name and role: "I'm Samuel, a product designer who also builds."
- Background: trained designer who works across Framer, Webflow, and Wix Studio. Uses modern tools including AI to ship faster than a traditional team.
- Why it matters: no handoffs means no miscommunication. Design intent is preserved all the way through to the final build.

**Remove the quote** "Most AI-generated sites look exactly the same." It positions you against AI while you also use AI — that creates doubt. Replace with something that speaks to your design standards without referencing AI at all. Something like: "Good design isn't decoration. It's how your business earns trust before you say a word."

**Update the three discipline cards:**

| Current | Change to |
|---------|-----------|
| Product Design | **Brand & Product Design** — UI/UX, visual systems, and brand identity |
| AI Development | **Web Development** — Framer, Webflow, Wix Studio, shipped in weeks |
| Automation | **Ongoing Design Partnership** — Retainer-based support for growing teams |

## Visual & UX Notes

- **Remove or heavily reduce scroll-hijacking animations.** The large empty gaps between sections slow visitors down. A founder evaluating your services wants to scan the page quickly — don't make them scroll through 15 screens of empty space to find pricing.
- **Keep the clean, light aesthetic.** It's professional and functional.
- **Bring more typographic confidence** — stronger type hierarchy, considered spacing, and restrained use of colour will signal that this studio takes design seriously.
- **Ensure the nav is sticky** so "Start a Project" is always one click away.
